# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-10-16)

**Not compatible with 0.1.0 video projects.** The lint and the scripts now
read `src/lib/layout.mjs` and `src/lib/palettes.mjs` from the video
project, which a 0.1.0 project does not have: make such a video again with
`new-video` (its `facts.md` and `script.json` can be copied over). The
storyboard takes `paletteLight` and `paletteDark`; a single `palette` key
is an error. Install choices saved by 0.1.0 still apply; the new keys take
their defaults until setup runs again.

### Added

- **Three formats**: 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1, all 1080 px on the short side.
  Claude picks the format from the subject (16:9 when unsure, and whenever
  a terminal or code line is too long for a narrow frame), or the user asks
  for one. Install key `format` (`auto` by default). Every scene, the
  captions and the chapter bar are laid out for the three; the lint checks
  line lengths, rows and sizes per format.
- **Squared-paper stages.** Light papers `giallo` (yellow notepad, the
  default), `quaderno`, `milli`, `arancio`, `tecnico`, `rosso`, `verde`,
  `grigio`; dark papers `notte` (faded black, the default) and `ardesia`.
  Install keys `paletteLight` and `paletteDark`, copied into the storyboard
  and checked by the lint. Contrast at least 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for
  marks in every palette.
- **Text font as an install choice** (`font`): the bundled Inter, a
  sans-serif found on the machine, or a font file. New script
  `scripts/fonts.mjs` (`list`, `check`). The chosen files are copied into
  each video and kept out of its git.
- **Code words in the captions** in JetBrains Mono: commands, file names
  and identifiers, recognised by the voice step (backticks, identifier
  shapes, paths, glossary terms in backticks). Contract B gets
  `words[].code`.
- **Music and sound effects**, both on by default, generated by the
  plugin's own code from a seed of the video's slug: no audio of anyone
  else, no download, the same video sounds the same every time. The music
  is ducked under the voice (16 LU under it by default, measured), rises in
  long pauses, changes with each chapter and ends with the film. Effects
  mark a chapter change and the key moments the storyboard names, always in
  a pause of the narration, never over a word. Install keys `music`,
  `musicStyle` (`warm`, `pulse`, `minimal`, or `auto`: Claude picks it from
  the subject, the default), `musicLevel` (`high`, the default, `normal`,
  `low`), `sfx`, `audioDir`; per-video overrides ("no music", "music
  lower", "more rhythm", "no effects").
- **A folder of one's own sounds** (`audioDir`): files named `music*`,
  `chapter*`, `error*`, `success*` replace the generated ones, kind by kind.
- New script `scripts/audio.mjs`: builds the music and effects alone and
  says where each effect falls.
- Storyboard: `parts` (a two-row chapter bar for many chapters) and the
  shot field `sfx` (`error`, `success` at a beat).
- Reports: an `audio` block in `qa/render.json`, `qa/audio.json`, the
  effects in `qa/lint.json`.

### Changed

- **Chapter bar**: fill only, every chapter always visible, nothing
  scrolls. When the labels do not fit one row (the lint measures them), the
  storyboard groups the chapters into parts: the parts below, the chapters
  of the current part above.
- **Between a light and a dark shot, a crossfade** (15 frames, 0.5 s at
  30 fps) instead of the
  push; the storyboard still says `push`. Push, wipe and fade-to-dark are
  unchanged elsewhere.
- **Pace**: an acronym weighs half a word per letter, at least one word
  (`SSO` 1.5), in the spoken words per second.
- On 9:16 and 1:1, terminal and code tiles may go down to 24 px (61
  characters a line, 65 in a full terminal); 28 px stays the minimum for
  everything else.
- `render.mjs` mixes the music and effects under the narration before the
  two loudness passes; the lint and `render.mjs` also check the width, the
  height and the palettes against `video.config.json`.
- `CodeWalk` stops its zoom where the longest line still fits;
  `StrikeReplace` is a centred block on every format.
- Contact sheets per shape; the page's player fits the window height, so a
  9:16 video is not taller than the screen.
- `new-video.mjs` adds `audio/` and `public/fonts/text/` to the video
  project's `.gitignore`.
- Setup asks for the light paper, the dark paper, the font, music,
  effects and the sound folder; it does not ask for the format, the music
  style or the music level.
- Documentation: the sources of the style and safety rules are written as
  what was seen in test runs.

### Removed

- The 0.1.0 palettes (light grey and dark navy): the stages are the
  papers above.
- The scrolling window of five chapters in the chapter bar.
- The limits "16:9 1080p only" and "no music, no sound effects".

### Fixed after the review, before release

- `config.mjs video` run again on a video (a change after delivery, such as
  "music lower") keeps the video's own overrides, the keys its
  `video.config.json` marks `override`, unless they are given again;
  `key=default` drops one. Before, every run started from `config.json`,
  so a 9:16 video came back as 16:9 and its music style changed. A value
  equal to `config.json`'s is recorded as `config`. The render's message on
  a width or height mismatch says to run `config.mjs video` with the
  video's format, not to copy the size into the storyboard.
- A file of `audioDir` that ffmpeg cannot decode no longer stops the
  render: it is a warning, and another file of its kind or the generated
  sound is used. The lint decodes the user's files first and plans the
  effects with their real lengths; `render.mjs` builds the music and
  effects before the render, and prints the effects of a one-shot render
  for that shot only.
- Captions and chapter-bar labels switch colour in the middle of a
  light/dark crossfade, not along it: they no longer turn grey on a grey
  stage (caption contrast at least 3.1:1 on every frame, every pair of
  papers).
- Code words in the captions keep their brackets and a final `?` or `!`
  (`main()`, `arr[0]`, `$?`); only `.`, `,` and `;` after them are the
  sentence's.
- Layout on 9:16 and 1:1: a long `StepList` item wraps to two lines at
  28 px instead of running out of the frame; a long `StrikeReplace`
  `after` shrinks to fit one line, and a prefix that does not share its
  line gets a line of its own; a `StatusCard` with a slab that does not fit
  the 1:1 stage draws the value smaller (down to 72 px); `HookStatus` node
  labels stay inside the text margin; a long code word in the captions
  shrinks to stay in the caption box. The lint checks each of these with
  the template's own arithmetic.
- On 9:16 and 1:1 the chapter bar's parts row is 54 px under the chapters
  row, on a ruling line of the default papers (it was 64 px, 10 px off).
- A font family that lacks most of the Latin alphabet (a symbol font) is
  refused, as a font file already was.
- Palette and beat names are looked up as own keys only (a name such as
  `toString` passed and crashed the theme).

### Fixed after the test videos, before release

- **The music has no sharp notes any more.** The bell notes of `minimal`,
  the electric piano of `warm` and the pluck and tick of `pulse` are gone:
  every layer starts softly (60 ms or more), and the styles differ by
  harmony, tempo, timbre and slow movement. In the test videos those notes
  rose 11 to 18 dB over the bed in 20 ms and were heard, in the pauses, as
  loud sound effects placed at random. `qa/render.json` and `qa/audio.json`
  report the sharpest note of the music (`transientDb`, limit 8 dB; the
  generated styles stay under 6.5); the lint and the render warn about a
  user's music file over the limit.
- **A key effect stays next to its word**: it starts at most 0.3 s after
  the word ends (the end the voice's energy shows) or ends at most 0.3 s
  before it starts, else it is left out with a lint warning that says
  where to anchor it. Before, it could wait up to 4 s and land on the next
  chapter's cut. Reports give each key effect's `fromWordMs`.
- **Fewer voice rounds.** A pace of 2.7-3.0 words per second is noted, not
  corrected (dense narrations measured 2.72-2.85 at the default speed);
  only over 3.0 does the video's speed go down. New `voice.mjs --estimate`:
  the expected pace and length before any synthesis (within 0.3 s on two
  test videos), shown at the narration checkpoint. Sentences changed after
  the checkpoint are listed in `OUTCOME.md` instead of asking again; at
  most two `--try` per term.
- Length: the time the subject needs; "short" sets no target and a phone
  video may run past a minute.
- Format: what counts is the lines the video shows, not every line of
  `facts.md`; a wide table's values fit a `StatusCard`. The lint's message
  for a line too long says what to do instead.
- `voice.mjs` refuses narration markup inside backticks (it was said
  aloud, letter by letter); a `{shown|spoken}` whose shown side is a
  command (`{kubectl rollout restart|ipa:...}`) is code in the captions. A
  colon stays with a code word (`env:`).
- Lint warnings: a terminal output line, or a focus on it, before the
  command above has finished typing; a `StrikeReplace` whose `atBefore`
  leaves the stage empty for more than 0.8 s.
- `CodeWalk`: the card is as wide as its code needs, and the push stops
  where the card and its band still fit between the frame margins (they
  ran off the right edge on 16:9 and 9:16).
- Terminal: the focus brackets sit in the gap between rows (they touched
  the line above at 24 px), and the prompt is there from the start.
  Dimmed lines of a band focus and of `CodeWalk` at 0.5 opacity (4.5:1
  for the ink, was 2.4:1).
- `config.mjs video --chosen <keys>` records Claude's choices as `claude`,
  apart from the user's (`override`); `--project` inside the videos folder
  uses the project above it; `set` leaves out values equal to the default,
  also ones saved earlier; `doctor` names the folder a stale model
  manifest points to.
- Page: an optional `source` for a section of prose only, and a warning for
  a number of a section's text with no source in that section.
- The `StrikeReplace` example no longer puts the new version in front of
  the old value (it stated a false fact until the strike).
- `pulse`'s table row: "unless the subject is delicate". The voice docs'
  brand example is YouTube.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- `/video-director:setup`: machine check (`doctor`), a Python venv for the
  voice step built at user level from pinned, hashed wheels, the Kokoro and
  Whisper models pinned by sha256 (or reused from an existing folder), and the
  install-time choices: language, voice with samples, captions, HTML page,
  checkpoints, video folder. Every choice can be overridden for one video.
- `/video-director:make`: facts with sources, narration with an optional
  checkpoint, bilingual voice (English terms with English phonemes in the same
  Kokoro voice) with a Whisper pronunciation check and a fix loop, a
  data-driven storyboard with word-anchored beats and a lint, stills and
  contact sheets, a freeze probe, a loopback-only render with process cleanup
  and loudness at -14 LUFS, a self-contained HTML page, a final review by a
  fresh sub-agent, and `OUTCOME.md`.
- A Remotion template (4.0.535, lockfile) with nine scene types (`HookStatus`,
  `Terminal`, `CodeWalk`, `FlowDiagram`, `StrikeReplace`, `StatusCard`,
  `StepList`, `Lesson`, `Screenshot`) plus a `Custom` escape hatch, a chapter
  bar that fills and scrolls, short captions, a camera on every shot, light and
  dark stages, and push, wipe and fade-to-dark transitions.
- A worked example: `plugins/video-director/examples/acme-shop-crashloop/`
  (fictional incident).

### Fixed after timed test runs, before release

- Delivery opens the result: a new install choice `openOnDelivery` (default on,
  overridable per video) and `scripts/open.mjs`, which opens the finished page,
  or the MP4 when there is no page, with the system's default program, once, at
  the end. Nothing is opened while the video is being made, and sub-agents never
  open anything.

- Template: a `HookStatus` slab value shrinks to fit its card (a five-digit
  value ran out of it beside a flow) and the caption wraps; a `StatusCard`
  row without `ok` has no mark (it showed a red cross); `StepList` and
  `StatusCard` are centred in the stage with larger type for 1-3 items, and
  `FlowDiagram` scales its whole drawing to fill the stage (up to 1.4x); the
  title of a list and the empty card are on screen from the first frame;
  `CodeWalk` draws line numbers only with `startLine`; `StrikeReplace` takes
  `atBefore`; between a light and a dark shot the theme colours switch at the
  hand-over of the push, so no mid-grey frame.
- Lint: values are found in `facts.md` without backticks, bold or case; the
  length is checked against the storyboard's `targetSeconds` when the user
  asked for a length; a slab alone needs one beat; warnings for a slab value
  drawn small, a list item that makes the list small, more than one red row,
  a strike after its replacement; errors for a centred slab next to a flow and
  for the template's placeholder `storyboard.json` and `voiceover.json` (also
  in stills, freeze and render).
- Voice: parts marked in the narration language (`{SSO|...}`, `{503|...}`,
  `{commit|it:commit}`) are checked like English terms and counted apart;
  English terms said in the narration language are listed; a spelled acronym
  counts as one word in the pace; `kubelet` is in the base lexicon; the
  summary points to the "Pace over 2.7" steps.
- `stills.mjs --shot` takes several shots, with one bundle and one browser.
- Config: `config.mjs set` and `show` warn about an absolute `videosDir`;
  `config.mjs video` takes `videosDir` from the video's folder; `show`
  prints `modelsFound`. `doctor` states the memory the render needs.
- Page: `values` for codes and names that are not commands; decisions with
  one option (a proposal) or none; warnings for sources, commands, values and
  numbers not found in `facts.md`.
- Skill: an example and the "decisions already taken are not open" rule for
  the task arc; a section on longer videos; what to do when the pace is over
  2.7; which stills to run after the review fix; render time 5-9 minutes.
- Example: the code close-up is titled "container checkout", as its facts
  say, without line numbers.
